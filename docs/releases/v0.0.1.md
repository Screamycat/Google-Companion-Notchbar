### Google-Companion Notchbar v0.0.1

- **Living Companion (BMO)**: An interactive animated companion that lives continuously inside your Notch across both Peek island and expanded states. Reacts dynamically with emotions, head pats, belly tickles, curiosity, study animations, and music-reactive vibing.
- **Dynamic DSP Audio Analyzer & Vibe Thresholds**: Real-time percussive transient detection and crest factor analysis on a 0–100 energy scale. Includes interactive threshold sliders for Level 2 (Groove) and Level 3 (Super Hype), real-time range needle, and dynamic active level badges.
- **Fluid 3-Stage Flow & Dynamic Morphing**: Smooth transitions between closed pill, peek island, and full notch view without glitching or jarring geometry snaps.
- **Redesigned Settings Structure**: Clean 5-page categorized organization:
  - **Companion**: Customization for 9 body shapes, color palettes, accessories, hats, eyewear, and audio vibe threshold sliders.
  - **Appearance**: Dynamic background visualizers (audio spectrum, aurora, synthwave grid, topographic contours, frosted glass).
  - **Size & Timing**: Unified dimensions, corner radiuses, and hover response timings.
  - **Tabs**: Modular tab reordering with custom groups (separated by clean `|` dividers in the dock).
  - **General**: Multi-monitor targeting, Windows autostart, and in-app updates.
- **Dedicated Update Channel**: In-app updates and release delivery powered directly through the `Screamycat/Google-NotchBar` repository.
