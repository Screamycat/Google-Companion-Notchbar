### Google-Companion Notchbar v0.2.0

- **Google Calendar Tab (Milestone 1)**:
  - Full Google Calendar integration in the Notch with single Google account session bridge (piggybacking on the Gemini login—zero additional authentication or setup required).
  - Interactive Month Grid View (6-week matrix, Monday-first, with per-calendar color event dots, today highlight, and active day selection).
  - Dynamic Agenda Timeline featuring "Upcoming" and "Day" modes, relative countdown badges ("Now", "in 15m", "in 2h"), location markers, and custom glass scrollbars.
  - 1-Click Universal Event Link Launcher: Supports video calls (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, Webex) and web destinations (lecture portals like Chalmers TimeEdit, course materials, attached links) with native system browser launching.
  - Mini-Island Peek Pill integration: Collapsed Notch displays upcoming event titles and live countdown timers, with a 1-click header toggle ("Pill: On / Off").
  - Calendar Management in Settings: Per-calendar toggle switches, inline calendar renaming (with save/cancel/reset), and customizable color accent palette picker.
- **Companion Living Emotions & Reactions Expansion**:
  - New `celebrate` companion mood: Joyful jumping animation, smiling 'D' face, and floating celebration confetti (`🎉`, `✦`, `✧`) for free calendar days.
  - Contextual day-click reactions: Busy days trigger `studying` with floating open book overlay; fewer events trigger `curious`; clicking events winks; clicking event links vibes.
  - Interactive companion poking: Clicking or petting the companion in the calendar tab triggers playful expressions (`happy`, `winking`, `tickled`, `petting`).
- **KaTeX Mathematical Formula Rendering**:
  - Full mathematical equation support in the Gemini AI assistant with inline math (`$...$`) and block equations (`$$...$$`).
- **Dynamic World Drizzle Physics & Polish**:
  - Realistic gentle drizzle weather physics with angled transparent droplets and volumetric mist.
  - Fixed Sun/Moon placement and responsive backdrop rendering.
- **Roadmap & Milestone Tracking**:
  - Added Milestone 8 (Linux Desktop Support) and Milestone 9 (AI Screen Vision & Visual Assistant) to repository roadmap.
