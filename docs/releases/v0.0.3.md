### Google-Companion Notchbar v0.0.3

- **Dynamic World Studio & Background Engine**:
  - Real-time weather and daylight sky simulation with live Open-Meteo synchronization (temperature, WMO weather codes, wind, and day/night transitions).
  - Astronomical Day/Night cycle with sun corona and cratered moon, positioned in the open upper sky arch so it is never obscured by widgets.
  - Soft atmospheric cloud and overcast diffusion — celestial bodies softly gleam through cloud decks instead of abruptly disappearing.
  - Generative weather particles: fluid rain & drizzle streaks with window condensation beads and wet puddle sheen, gentle snowflakes, rolling ground mist, lightning flashes, and autumn wind leaves.
  - Customizable horizons: Alpine mountain silhouette, layered 8-bit Metropolis skyline with illuminated windows & antenna beacon, rolling ocean swells, and Ghibli canopy treehouse.
  - Dedicated Studio customization panel in Settings → Appearance with one-click atmosphere presets.
- **Unified Global Weather Pipeline**:
  - Built a centralized reactive `forecastStore` singleton with in-flight request deduplication and 10-minute cache validation, ensuring 100% synchronization across all widgets and live previews.
- **Background Effects Library & Scaling**:
  - Categorized into *All*, *Music* (music-reactive visualizers), *Moving*, and *Static* with a distinguished `✦ Studio` badge.
  - True responsive canvas scaling: no stretching or deformation when expanding or morphing the notch island dimensions.
  - Renamed BMO Retro to "Circuits" and added vibrant accent-color lighting.
  - Replaced harsh fog dots with a rolling harmonic volumetric ground mist blanket.
- **Living Companion in Tools**:
  - Dual-mode companion integrated into Clipboard and Converter tabs with live emotional feedback and ambient cursor tracking.
